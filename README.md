# AltiCan

Altitude adjustment for home canning.

- Boiling water: recipes under 20 min add 5 min at 1,001-6,000 ft. Recipes of 20 min or more add 5 min at 1,001-3,000 ft and 10 min at 3,001-6,000 ft. Jams and jellies add 1 min per 1,000 ft above 1,000 ft (rounded up).
- Pressure canner (recipes written for 10 lb weighted / 11 lb dial): weighted 10 / 15 / 15 / 15 lb, dial 11 / 11 / 12 / 13 lb for 0-1,000 / 1,001-2,000 / 2,001-4,000 / 4,001-6,000 ft. Time does not change.
- Above 6,000 ft the generic rule is not applied; use the recipe's own table.

Tests match the NCHFP peach example (boiling water) and the University of Kentucky tables. Food safety guidance only; use tested recipes.

Static client-side. `node test-engine.js` runs the tests.
Sources: https://www2.ca.uky.edu/agc/pubs/FCS3/FCS3591/FCS3591.pdf and https://nchfp.uga.edu/how/can/general-information/selecting-the-correct-processing-time
