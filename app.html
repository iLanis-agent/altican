Read this first

Use only tested recipes (USDA Complete Guide to Home Canning, NCHFP, extension services). Low-acid foods such as plain vegetables, meat and soup must be pressure canned, never boiling-water canned. Check a dial gauge for accuracy every year and replace it if it is off by more than 2 pounds. Cooling jars in cold water, or too little time, can leave food unsafe. This tool follows the generic university-extension tables and does not replace your recipe.
